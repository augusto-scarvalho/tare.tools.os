# EXP-3: locality and loading cost

The bounded measurement belongs to Local Labs: [maintained result](https://github.com/augusto-scarvalho/tare.tools.local-labs/blob/master/docs/research/LOCALITY_COST_QUALIFICATION.md),
[machine-readable summary](https://github.com/augusto-scarvalho/tare.tools.local-labs/blob/master/docs/research/LOCALITY_COST_QUALIFICATION.json)
and [offline replay tool](https://github.com/augusto-scarvalho/tare.tools.local-labs/blob/master/tools/analysis/locality_study.py).

Six public TUI read-only sessions used existing Qwen38-GSQ/RTX3090 and GPT-6.1 Sol
routes. Local model loading took 12.11 s; it is already included in measured wall
time. A healthy resident PID and prompt-cache counters are separate evidence.
Both warm local trials completed near the cloud route's latency in these two
source-reading tasks. All substantive diagnoses were source-reviewed; only 2/6
met the requested word target. The initial private screen's citation/keyword
failures are preserved, with offline rescoring and no inference replay.

GPU energy was sampled gross, including a separately reported campaign idle
window. Total monetary cost remains unknown without whole-host, electricity,
hardware and subscription allocation. No zero-cost, broad quality or routing
winner is established by this pilot.

`res-exp-locality` records completion of the operator's measurement slice with
grade C and its original research scope retained in history. The historical
automatic-placement/70%-savings proposal stays unadmitted. No scheduler change,
model download, cache reset, service restart or production-model promotion is
part of this delivery; normal model residency/cache are preserved.

Reuse the offline reader for ordinary observations. This completed measurement
does not schedule new inference or a recurring paid benchmark.
Private raw evidence has a 64 MiB ceiling and a 2026-11-20 retention review. The
small maintained owner result and this OS pointer remain the restart destination.
